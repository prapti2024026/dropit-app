DropIt

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026

DropIt is a student research project that lets you send files to another DropIt user with hand gestures (GRAB to send, DROP to receive). This page explains what we collect and why.

What we collect

  • Account details: your username and email address. If you sign up with email, your password is stored only as a salted bcrypt hash, never in plain text.
  • Google sign-in: if you choose "Continue with Google", Google shares your name, email address, profile picture and a Google account ID with us. We use them only to create and sign in to your DropIt account. We do not get your Google password or access to your Gmail, Drive or any other Google data.
  • Files you send through the server (up to 200 MB): the file, its name, size, type and a SHA-256 checksum, plus who sent it to whom and when.
  • Direct transfers (bigger files): the file goes straight from the sender's browser to the receiver's browser over an encrypted WebRTC connection and never reaches our server. We only see the file's name, size and type, the connection details needed to link the two browsers (which include network addresses), and the SHA-256 checksum reported when it arrives.

Camera

Hand gestures are recognised on your own device by MediaPipe running in your browser. Camera video is never uploaded, recorded or stored.

How we use it

  • To sign you in and keep your session (a token stored in your browser).
  • To deliver files only to the receiver you choose, and to show your transfer history.
  • To check that a file arrived unchanged (SHA-256).

We do not sell your data, show ads, or share it with anyone except the services that run DropIt: MongoDB Atlas (database and file storage), Render (server) and Netlify (website).

How long we keep it

  • Files stored on the server are removed 24 hours after the receiver drops them, or after 7 days if nobody does. The history entry stays, marked as removed.
  • Connection details for direct transfers are kept in memory only, for at most a few hours.
  • Your account stays until you ask us to delete it.

Your choices

You can delete pending transfers from your History page. To delete your account and all of its data, or to ask what we hold about you, email praptir.2002@gmail.com.

Security

Connections use HTTPS, passwords are hashed, login attempts are rate limited and only the sender and receiver of a file can download it. No system is perfectly secure, so please do not use DropIt for highly sensitive files.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update the date at the top of this page.
